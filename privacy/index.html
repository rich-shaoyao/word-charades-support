Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cluvio ("we", "our", "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information we collect

The App does not require an account, and we do not ask you to provide any personal information to play. The words you type exist only on your device for the duration of the game; they are never uploaded, and we never see them. The App runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests of any kind.

2. No advertising, no third-party SDKs

Cluvio contains no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics or tracking SDK. We do not show ads, we do not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not ask for tracking permission because no tracking takes place.

3. Permissions

The App does not require microphone, camera, location, contacts or notification permissions to play.

4. Children

Cluvio does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

5. Data retention and security

We do not operate servers for this game and we do not store your personal data. Your only stored information is the game settings you choose (round length and number of words), which are kept locally on your device and can be removed at any time by deleting the App.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact us
