Flip Chess — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Flip Chess iOS application ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. It applies to the App and to this website.

Summary

Flip Chess collects no personal information. The App has no account system, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. It does not connect to the internet. Everything happens on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number or any other contact details
  • Account credentials — there is no sign-up or login
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA) or fingerprints
  • Usage, analytics, crash or diagnostic data sent to us or to any third party
  • Photos, contacts, microphone, camera or any other content on your device

In App Store terms, the App’s privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Data stored on your device

Your game settings — such as the selected difficulty and the sound toggle — are kept only in the App’s own local storage on your device. They are never uploaded, and they are removed when you delete the App. A game in progress exists only in memory while you play it.

Advertising and third parties

Flip Chess contains no advertising. There is no AdMob, no mediation network, no consent-management SDK and no analytics library of any kind. Because the App does not track you across apps or websites, it does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and it does not request or use the advertising identifier.

Permissions

The App requests no iOS permissions. It does not access the network, the microphone, the camera, your photo library, your contacts or your location.

Purchases

Flip Chess is free and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. We do not receive or process any payment information.

Children’s privacy

Flip Chess is a board game suitable for general audiences. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents should note that no data leaves the device at all.

Data retention and security

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain, secure or delete on our side. Any settings the App stores live on your own device, protected by the security of iOS, and are deleted along with the App.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how data is handled — for example, if advertising is ever introduced — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
