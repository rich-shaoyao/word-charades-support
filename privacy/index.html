Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Charades ("we", "our", "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information we collect

The App itself does not require an account, and we do not ask you to provide any personal information to play. The game content runs on your device and does not require an internet connection.

2. Advertising (third-party services)

Word Charades is free and supported by advertising served by third-party ad networks, including Google AdMob. These partners may collect and process certain device data (such as the advertising identifier, device type, and usage data) to deliver and measure ads, and — where you grant permission — to serve personalized ads.

We use the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Brazil). You can decline consent at any time; the game remains fully playable.

3. Tracking and your choices

On first launch, the App may ask for permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). You may choose "Ask App Not to Track" or decline. You can also change your choice later in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings at any time.

4. Permissions

The App does not require microphone, camera, location or contacts permissions to play.

5. Children

Word Charades does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising shown to users who identify as children is non-personalized, in accordance with applicable policies.

6. Data retention and security

We do not operate servers for this game and do not store your personal data. Advertising data is handled by the ad networks under their own privacy policies (see Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
