Cluvio — Support

Thank you for playing Cluvio! If you have questions, feedback, or run into any issue, this page will help you.

How to play

Type a single word on your phone and keep the screen to yourself. Tap Start Timer, then describe the word out loud — say anything except the word itself. Your teammates shout their guesses; tap Correct when someone gets it, or Skip to move on. Your score is how many words were guessed before you run out of words.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Can I choose my own words?
Yes — that is the main way to play. You type the word yourself before each round. If you cannot think of one, tap Surprise me to pick a random word from the built-in word bank.

Q: How long is a round, and how many words do I get?
Both are yours to set on the home screen. Round time can be 10, 20, 30 or 60 seconds per word, or any value from 5 to 300 seconds. A game can be 5, 10, 15 or 20 words, or any count from 1 to 50.

Q: Are there ads in Cluvio?
No. Cluvio has no ads at all — the app contains no advertising SDK and no tracking of any kind. It is simply free to download.

Q: Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Cluvio never connects to the internet — it works fully offline and collects no data.

Q: Why is there no sound or voice feature?
Cluvio is a spoken, face-to-face party game — the clues are given out loud, so audio and speech features are not needed.
