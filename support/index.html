How to play

All 32 standard Xiangqi points are filled. Both Generals start face-up on their own back-rank centre; the other 30 pieces are face-down and shuffled together, Red and Black mixed, so you never know who stands where or whose piece it is. Long-press any piece for about half a second to see its English name and how it moves.

On your turn you step one of your pieces. There is no flip-in-place: a hidden piece flips face-up the moment it finishes its move. A face-down piece moves according to the standard piece of the point it occupies — on a Chariot point it slides like a Chariot, on a Soldier point it steps one square forward — and once revealed it follows its real identity.

Capture the enemy General to win. A side with no legal move loses, and 60 moves each with no capture and no flip is a draw.

Frequently asked questions

Q: How is this different from the “flip chess” I know?
Classic flip chess (暗棋) is decided by rank — General beats Advisor, Advisor beats Elephant, and so on. Flip Chess keeps the hidden board but removes the ranking: a piece can be taken when a move can reach it and its identity allows it. Placement is luck; the game is skill.

Q: Why can’t I flip a piece where it sits?
There is deliberately no flip-in-place action. Revealing a piece always costs you a move, which is what makes pushing a hidden piece the central decision of the game.

Q: Why can’t I capture the face-down piece next to my own General?
Hidden pieces in your own half can never be captured, no matter who they really belong to. You deal with them by pushing them out. Hidden pieces in the opponent’s half, on the other hand, can be captured by either side.

Q: Can I choose the difficulty?
Yes. On the main menu pick Easy, Normal or Hard, then tap Play vs Computer. For a game against someone sitting next to you, tap Two Players.

Q: Does the app need an internet connection?
No. Flip Chess works entirely offline and never makes a network request.

Q: Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no account to create.

Q: How do I remind myself how a piece moves?
Long-press a piece for about half a second to see its English name and a short description of its move. The full rules are under How to Play on the main menu.
