Word Charades — Support

Thank you for playing Word Charades! If you have questions, feedback or run into any issue, this page will help you.

How to play

One player holds the phone and sees a secret word. Describe it or act it out without saying the word itself. Your teammates guess; tap Correct on a hit or Skip to move on. Score as many words as you can before the countdown ends.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Why does the app ask for tracking permission?
Word Charades is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. With your permission, the app may share the advertising identifier with ad partners to show more relevant ads. You can decline — the game remains fully playable.

Q: Why is there no sound / voice feature?
Word Charades is a spoken, face-to-face party game — audio and speech features are not required to play.

Q: Can I add my own words?
Yes. The app supports custom word packs that you create on your own device.

Q: Does the game need an internet connection?
No. The game itself works offline; an internet connection is only needed to load ads.
