Cluvio

One word, one clue-giver, one clock. Describe it any way you like — just don't say it — and let your team guess.

How to play

  1. Set the word. Type a single word and keep the screen to yourself — nobody else should read it.
  2. Hit Start Timer. The clock starts the moment you tap, and it runs for the whole word.
  3. Describe it. Explain the word out loud. Say anything except the word itself.
  4. Correct or Skip. Tap Correct when someone guesses it. Tap Skip to move on.
  5. Only correct counts. Your final score is how many words were guessed. A skip doesn't score, but it does use up one of your words.

Features

✍️ Your own words

Every round starts with a word you type yourself — inside jokes, tricky ones, anything your group loves.

🎲 Surprise me

Stuck for ideas? Tap Surprise me to pull a random word from the built-in word bank.

⏱ Adjustable round time

Pick 10, 20, 30 or 60 seconds per word, or set any length from 5 to 300 seconds.

🎯 Choose how many words

Play 5, 10, 15 or 20 words a game — or set any count from 1 to 50.

👥 Two or more players

One person gives the clues, everyone else guesses. Ideal for parties and family nights.

📴 Plays fully offline

The game needs no internet connection at all — Cluvio works fully offline, always.

🔒 Privacy-friendly

No account, no sign-up, no tracking, and your words never leave your device. Read our Privacy Policy.
